# What is ADAS?

Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) are vehicle systems that observe the driving environment, assist the driver, and—in some functions—intervene through steering, braking, or acceleration.

## Typical Functions

- Lane-departure warning and lane keeping
- Adaptive cruise control
- Forward-collision warning and automatic emergency braking
- Blind-spot monitoring
- Parking assistance

## Core Idea

An ADAS feature follows a recurring pattern:

```text
Sense ? Understand ? Decide ? Act
```

Sensors collect information; perception turns it into objects, lanes, and free space; decision and planning select a safe response; control sends vehicle commands.
